# Rock texture sources

The rock kit's painted layers (`src/render/rockMaterial.ts`): each carries its painting's value round its mean (red) and
its relief as a normal map (green and blue), made tileable layers by `tools/rock_textures.py` from paintings generated
with Codex, kept in `D:\dragonbound-archive\codex\tex\rock`. The zone's rock and moss colours give the hue.

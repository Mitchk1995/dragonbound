# Leaf texture sources

The grown trees' sourced leaves (`src/render/foliage.ts`; the oak's and the common tree's are painted in code). Each
was generated with Codex image generation as one softly painted spray on a clear ground, then laid out by
`tools/bark_textures.py` as a 3 x 3 atlas of that spray (each cell turned, mirrored, sized and toned a little
differently) with its colours made multipliers of the game's leaf colours. The generated sources are kept in
`D:\dragonbound-archive\codex\tex\leaves` (the bushes' in `D:\dragonbound-archive\codex\tex\nature-leaves`).

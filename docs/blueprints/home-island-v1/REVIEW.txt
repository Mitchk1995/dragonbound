DRAGONBOUND HOME ISLAND v1 - PROPOSAL - NOT IMPLEMENTED / OPUS-AUTHORED - CASTLE SITE PROVISIONAL

CONCEPT
A torn crown-spur fragment. The north and west edges are fresh, angular tear faces, and a fresh unbridged
fracture notch cuts in from the north. The east and south edges are older and weathered, with rounded lobes
and a headland. The island floats over a sky void. There is no sea; the only water is a spring, a stream and
a pond, and the stream falls off the south edge.

SIZE
The proposal is 270 x 216 cells (bounding box) with 44,938 cells of land, about 3.2x the current land. The
current island is a nominal ~129 x 129 superellipse (~14,200 cells) on a 150x150 grid that is not all ground.

CASTLE SITE (provisional; nothing approved)
The castle-v2 footprint is placed by a rigid translation of (+28, +12) with rotation 0 and no scaling.
Validation measured 0 error in the pairwise curtain distances. It sits on the NW crown spur at h +11. Its
SE gatehouse already faces down to the settlement and the south camera, so no rotation was needed. Curtain
walls and towers stand at least 3.87 cells inside the crown edge.
- The postern leads to a new cliff stair down to the farm.
- Small yard furnishings are omitted; the training-yard overlap is being corrected in a separate castle-v2 task.
- No internal castle change was forced by this site.

ROUTES
The approach climbs in two ramp stages (h 0 -> +5 -> +11), about 75 cells. It leaves the court past the
Emberdeep Mine slot instead of passing through the portal ring.

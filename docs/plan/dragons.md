## October 3

- **Dragons, round 2 notes** (October 3, `D:\dragonbound-archive\notes\dragons-round2`): Cinderwing's claws are bigger than its foot pads, its chest piece looks wrong and its tail is chunky and badly shaped; the drakeling's face is weak, with a stray extra shape on the cheek. Now in the game (round 3, awaiting his verdict): both dragons stand in paws, a chunky paw block with three toe knuckles and a smaller blunt claw growing out of each toe (Cinderwing's ivory claws under dark toes, the drakeling's bigger cream claws), and Cinderwing's gilt anklets sit level round the leg just above the paw (its hind lower legs now drop straight into the paws). Cinderwing's flat ivory bib is replaced by ivory scutes that wrap the front of its chest from under the throat down between the forelegs, narrowing as they go and running on under the belly, crossed by the breast strap with a square gilt buckle at each corner; its throat glows through a network of furnace cracks. Its tail is six segments in one smooth taper from the hips to a fine tip, with no steps at the joints, drooping from the hips and lifting toward the gilt cuff and ivory spike, red crystals shrinking along its top; when it rears for the tail slam the tail rides up behind instead of sinking into the ground. The drakeling's head is one solid skull: a heavy brow over an eye sunk in its socket under a dark brow line, a long squared snout with two square nostrils, and a deep cream jaw; the cheek lump is gone. `tests/dragon-art.test.ts` checks the paws, scutes, tail taper and one-piece skull, and that the tails stay off the ground in every pose.

Newer decisions take precedence over older ones. Record each new decision here, dated, in the same session it is made.

## October 3

- **Dragons, round 2 notes** (October 3, `D:\dragonbound-archive\notes\dragons-round2`): Cinderwing's claws are bigger than its foot pads, its chest piece looks wrong and its tail is chunky and badly shaped; the drakeling's face is weak, with a stray extra shape on the cheek. Round 3 (his verdict is the next entry): both dragons stand in paws, a chunky paw block with three toe knuckles and a smaller blunt claw growing out of each toe (Cinderwing's ivory claws under dark toes, the drakeling's bigger cream claws), and Cinderwing's gilt anklets sit level round the leg just above the paw (its hind lower legs now drop straight into the paws). Cinderwing's flat ivory bib is replaced by ivory scutes that wrap the front of its chest from under the throat down between the forelegs, narrowing as they go and running on under the belly, crossed by the breast strap with a square gilt buckle at each corner; its throat glows through a network of furnace cracks. Its tail is six segments in one smooth taper from the hips to a fine tip, with no steps at the joints, drooping from the hips and lifting toward the gilt cuff and ivory spike, red crystals shrinking along its top; when it rears for the tail slam the tail rides up behind instead of sinking into the ground. The drakeling's head is one solid skull: a heavy brow over an eye sunk in its socket under a dark brow line, a long squared snout with two square nostrils, and a deep cream jaw; the cheek lump is gone. `tests/dragon-art.test.ts` checks the paws, scutes, tail taper and one-piece skull, and that the tails stay off the ground in every pose.
- **Dragons, round 3 verdict and round 4** (October 3; round 3 beside its sheets in `D:\dragonbound-archive\review\dragons-round3`). Mitchell kept round 3's paws, Cinderwing's chest scutes and tail taper and the drakeling's clean cheek, but not the drakeling's proportions: "too skinny", a "long head and skinny body", "head shape weak". Beside its sheet the drakeling was spindly where the sheet is chunky. Now in the game (round 4, awaiting his verdict): the drakeling is rebuilt to its sheet's mass. Its head is a short, deep wedge, about 1.25 times as long as it is deep (round 3's was twice): a tall cranium under a heavy brow block with a sloped front, a snout sloping down to a blunt nose with the nostrils high on its front, a bigger eye sunk in its socket under the brow, and a deep cream jaw. A thick neck rises steeply out of the chest, its cream throat running down into cream scutes on the chest, crossed by the leather collar and its gilt buckle; the body is a broad, deep barrel; the legs are thick, with heavy shoulders and thighs and chunky forearms long enough to show under the body, as on the sheet, standing at the chest's sides in bigger paws; the tail is thicker, with a cream underside, drooping from the hips and lifting toward the arrowhead; dark plates run up the neck, along the back and down the tail; and the wings are rooted on the shoulder tops and stand a little higher, the leading finger rising up and back as on the sheet. Cinderwing was bulked to its sheet's massive build as Claude's call, not one of his notes (he OK'd it): the whole torso, with its harness, scutes and cracks, grown broader and deeper, bigger rounded shoulders and haunches, legs about a third thicker, standing taller so the forearms and gilt anklets show as on the sheet, in paws grown with them, a shorter, thicker neck so the head sits close over the chest, and much bigger swept-back ivory horns; its round 3 paws, scutes and tail are kept. Both dragons' wings still stand lower than on the sheets, because the flight flap would carry raised wings over the back. `tests/dragon-art.test.ts` adds checks for the drakeling's short, deep head and its broad, deep body on thick legs, and that both dragons stand in paws wider than their legs.
- **Drakeling round 4 approved** (October 3, decision page: "yes, good"). The drakeling is done as built.
- **Cinderwing round 4 approved** (October 3, decision page: "yes, good"). The bulk-up and bigger horns stand; Cinderwing is done as built.

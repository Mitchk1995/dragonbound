# Combat and gathering pacing

Combat XP follows the equipped weapon style. There is no stance selector: every earned combat XP point trains that style, adds one third to Hitpoints and one half to Defence. Armour absorption contributes a small additional Defence reward (0.1 XP per damage absorbed). Save version 3 removes the former stance field while preserving existing progress.

Enemy XP is fixed per enemy and shared according to damage dealt. Mining uses half the ore's listed action XP; Smithing uses the full recipe XP. Chapter 1 assumes self-supplied bars: mining, smelting and smithing all take time.

## What these numbers establish

The deterministic simulators in `tests/balance/` measure efficient clears using current Chapter 1 content, equipment and abilities. The tables below are generated by `tests/balance.test.ts` using `BALANCE_REPORT`. They are modelled rates, not a human playtest or a promise of actual completion time. Exploration, mistakes, banking and individual play patterns can change the result.

Chapter 1 alone is not a complete level-99 content ladder. Its best measured rates flatten around combat/Mining level 35 and self-supplied Smithing level 53. The later-chapter rows assume that future content increases the best XP/hour by 4% per level after those points. Under that assumption one combat style reaches 99 in about 237 efficient hours, Mining in 175 and self-supplied Smithing in 377. No later-chapter content is implemented by that projection.

The mana checks cover ordinary clears and the prolonged Cinderwing fight. The melee checks cover retreating kobolds, including a comparison with the old stationary wind-up. These checks do not establish that the game already delivers the desired kiting feel.

## Next design iteration

The user wants Diablo II-led combat with richer ability use and OSRS-style long-term grinds. Kiting is an explicit goal: monster pursuit should leave room to reposition. Larger, more open exploratory regions, gathering inside primary adventure regions, and an initial level 1-10 region are tentative ideas for a later playable iteration. They have not been hardcoded into this recovery.

Review a small playable checkpoint before changing pursuit speeds, map scale or gathering placement. Use hands-on feedback alongside these measurements.
